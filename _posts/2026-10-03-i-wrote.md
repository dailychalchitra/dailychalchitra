I wrote a poem
I don't know how
Words started appearing on paper
Without waiting too much
Showing me they want to be visible.

I wrote a poem
Coming from emotions
That wanted to come out
That wanted to outburst
I wrote, I used a pen as my witness.

The pen gave ink to the poem
And the paper gave a printing space
Did I write or did I sent a testimony
To the whole world?

I wrote a poem
When my soul spoke out loud...
